Privacy on konf.app

This page covers the konf.app landing page and its "Request an invite" form. Conference sites on Konf have their own privacy page, written for that conference.

The invite form

When you send the form, we store:

  • your name and email address;
  • the conference name, the expected number of attendees, and whether it sells tickets;
  • your message, if you write one;
  • when you sent it, and the status of your request (new, invited or declined).

We use this only to answer your request and, if you get one, to send you an invite code. The form is sent to the Konf API at api.konf.app. Your IP address and email are used to limit how many requests one sender can make; the IP address is not stored with your request.

Only the people who run Konf can see the requests. We do not sell or share them, and we do not add you to a newsletter.

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email hei@konf.app. We delete every request from your email address when you ask.

Visiting the page

konf.app sets no cookies and has no analytics or tracking. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions

Write to hei@konf.app. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian data protection authority.
